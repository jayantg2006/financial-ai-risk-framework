# Intended-use policy

## Permitted

- Preliminary research on approved annual reports and financial disclosures.
- Locating relevant passages and comparing reported values.
- Drafting evidence-linked notes for analyst review.

## Restricted or prohibited

- Autonomous credit, lending, underwriting, investment, or counterparty decisions.
- Use with confidential, personal, or unauthorised documents.
- Treating generated interpretation as a verified fact without source review.
- Bypassing abstention by asking the system to invent or infer unavailable data.
- Representing benchmark performance as regulatory approval or universal reliability.

## Escalation

Escalate unsupported claims, wrong-year retrieval, citation mismatch, prompt injection, material numerical error, and any output that could change a financial decision.
